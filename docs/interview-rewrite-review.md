Approved 2026-11-14 and recorded, with the same voices, plain standard English and no accents added. Two changes came after approval:
- Every witness is now met at or before the first lock where they testify, including twists and bank questions. A validator rule checks this. The lock numbers below are the final ones.
- Katrina's Room 9 lie moved to p046. The lock 5 twist already uses p045, and two claims can't share one proof line.

**What changes**
- Each room has **one question**, asked of all three witnesses.
- Each witness is **met inside the room**, at the lock shown, before that lock's reading and never after the lock whose line checks them.
- Every statement is **one sentence**. The proof lines stay the same except in two rooms.
- **Room 4, Katrina:** her lines no longer rest on the paragraph describing her dress (p022). Both now use the paragraph about her father's farm (p023).
- **Room 9, Brom and Katrina:** both used p048, which carries a content note. That has been live since the Interview was built; the validator now checks Interview lines against content notes too. Brom moves to p043, Katrina to p045.

All 42 witnesses pass the checks: no statement repeats words from its proof line, every line is in its room, none is content-noted, and each witness is met in time.
