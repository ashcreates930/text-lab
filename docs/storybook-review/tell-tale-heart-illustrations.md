## Art style (one style for the whole story)

- One style for every page: a painterly storybook illustration in muted gouache on warm cream paper, with soft edges, visible brush texture, and deep shadow.
- Palette: charcoal, sepia, and warm cream, with terracotta and dim lantern amber as the only warm accents. Night scenes stay dark but readable.
- Setting: the bedroom ("chamber") of an old house at night: a bed, a door with a latch, closed shutters, plank floorboards. Nothing the text does not name.
- The narrator: never show a face. Show only a shadowed silhouette, the back or side of a head, a hand, or a sleeve in plain dark clothing. The text never describes how the narrator looks.
- The old man: elderly, thin, in plain nightclothes, features kept soft and mostly in shadow, except his "pale blue eye, with a film over it" when the page shows it.
- The lantern: a small closed tin "dark lantern" with a hinged shutter that lets out a thin beam.
- Never: words, letters, or numbers in the image; blood, wounds, weapons, or the body; detailed or frightening faces; anything a later page reveals.
