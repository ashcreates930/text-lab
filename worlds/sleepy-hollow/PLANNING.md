# Planning: The Legend of Sleepy Hollow

- **Author/year:** Washington Irving, 1820
- **Genre:** short story
- **Grade band:** 6 to 8
- **Setting:** The Empty Schoolhouse in the Hudson Valley, the morning after Ichabod Crane fails to return.
- **Possible roles:** New Schoolteacher; Traveling Reporter; Town Historian
- **Speaking and non-speaking characters:** Hans Van Ripper (male), Brom Bones (male) and Katrina Van Tassel (female) speak, in plain standard English. Each can be asked questions only after the student has met them on a storybook page, and each knows only the pages the student has reached. Katrina speaks as a witness in the Interview (approved by the teacher on 2026-10-31): everything she says rests on what Irving's text shows about her (the heiress, the coquette, the party, Brom's courtship and her favor toward Ichabod), and she adds no new plot. Ichabod Crane, Baltus Van Tassel, and the Headless Horseman do not speak; students learn about them through the text and artifacts only.
- **Shared goal:** Figure out what really happened to Ichabod.
- **Scene 1 artifact candidates:** The empty schoolhouse; Ichabod’s bundle and belongings; Cotton Mather’s history of witchcraft; hat and shattered pumpkin found near brook and bridge.
- **Scene 1 DOK 3 prompt:** The searchers found a hat and a shattered pumpkin. Ichabod owned a book about witchcraft. Which explanation does the evidence support better: a ghost or a trick? What can’t you prove yet?
- **Content notes:** None identified.
- **Teacher preview required:** false
- **Source edition:** Project Gutenberg edition of The Legend of Sleepy Hollow in The Sketch Book of Geoffrey Crayon, Gent.; verify exact ebook number and edition.

Preview scope: one playable scene only. The full build requires role briefings, passage panel, narration, generated art and audio, artifact questions, and an end screen that retains the Case File.
